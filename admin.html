Quantity bands and source dates

Both quantity endpoints are inclusive; a blank maximum means no upper limit. Supplier cost bands are optional: one fixed cost is supported. Header-only costs.csv is allowed; missing costs show unavailable margins. Upload a current snapshot: future dates and overlapping schedules are rejected. Source dates are provenance, not automatic activation dates.
